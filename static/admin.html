Escalation rules

When the assistant should stop and point the caller to emergency care or a human.

Custom escalation keywords
Any question containing one of these words is escalated, in addition to the built-in rules below.

Built-in rules

  1. Critical symptoms

    Chest pain, heart attack, breathing difficulty, unconsciousness, seizure, stroke and similar words in any supported language. Always escalated.

  2. Chest or breathing complaints

    Medical questions that mention the chest or breathing.

  3. Low-confidence medical query

    The question looks medical but the intent match is weak, so it needs clarification.

  4. Several urgency cues

    Multiple urgency keywords together with a medical question.

  5. Repeated question

    The caller asks the same thing again in one session, which may mean they are struggling.
